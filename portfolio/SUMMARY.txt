DESIGN CONCEPT — PIANO ROOM

A self-initiated website concept for a fictional UK piano school, created by
Silverside Web Solutions. The brief was to make starting piano feel inviting
while giving the school an identity rooted in music rather than a generic
education template.

The Living Score visual direction combines paper-white space, ink-black type,
vermilion accents, oversized editorial headlines and an original architectural
illustration. A playable piano lets visitors leave a visual trace on the page;
replay and a short guided phrase turn the opening into a musical experience.

Implemented features include responsive lesson pathways, keyboard and touch
piano controls, a switch for character-key shortcuts, a five-note guided lesson,
composition replay/reset, accessible FAQs and a clearly labelled enquiry demo.
The enquiry uses preset choices and produces a sample response and optional
text download. It sends no messages, makes no bookings, takes no payments and
requests no personal information.

This is a design concept, not commissioned client work. No trading school,
teacher credentials, customer testimonials or measured results are claimed.
The piano is synthesised; the visual score is expressive rather than formal
sheet-music transcription.
