PIANO ROOM — LOCAL CREATIVE REDESIGN

Inspection
The existing site was one buildless HTML page with inline CSS and JavaScript.
There were no internal pages, framework, database or installed dependencies.
Preserved section routes: #lessons, #approach, #faq. Preserved the playable
piano, all three lesson pathways, FAQ content and downloadable lesson enquiry.
No production deployment or hosting changes were made for this redesign.

Three creative directions considered
1. The Concert Programme: theatrical black-and-white layouts and programme
   typography. Strong cultural fit and easy implementation, but less welcoming
   and less interactive than the alternatives.
2. The Practice Room: architectural spatial composition and intimate tactile
   detail. Distinctive and suitable, but a fully immersive room would make
   mobile navigation and implementation disproportionately complex.
3. The Living Score: an editorial composition built around making music.
   Strong visual impact, high usability and feasible in plain HTML/CSS/JS.
   Selected because visitors participate in the identity and experience a
   small first lesson, connecting its originality directly to the school.

Identity and composition
Paper, ink and vermilion; large plain typography in dialogue with italic type;
asymmetric line breaks; small programme annotations. Rhythm is expressed
through changes of scale, density and spacing. The live score, programme-like
lesson entries and original architectural SVG all derive from the concept.
No stock photography, external fonts, paid services or added dependencies.
System fonts make this version entirely self-contained and fast to load.

Changed components
Homepage opening, navigation, interactive piano and visual score, guided
five-note lesson, replay/reset, lesson pathways, approach, interlude, FAQs,
closing enquiry, footer and enquiry modal. Split CSS and JS into local files.
No internal pages were added simply to expand the scope.

Creative refinement
Reviewed the full desktop and phone compositions. Corrected mobile headline
overflow and joined words from hidden breaks. Scaled the score to the device.
Retained navigation on phones; adapted lessons to a compact two-line programme.
Adjusted keyboard shortcuts to work after a piano button receives focus.

QA performed
Browser inspection at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 pixels. No horizontal page overflow.
Piano clicks, physical keyboard shortcuts, score generation, replay and reset.
Guided phrase completed with C E G E C and success feedback verified.
Lesson enquiry selection, form submission, downloaded text file and edit flow.
FAQ expansion and hash navigation. No browser console errors observed.
JavaScript syntax checked. Native semantic controls, labelled modal and inputs,
focus indication, focus return, Escape dismissal and reduced-motion CSS.

Limits
School is fictional. Real contact details, pricing, teachers and schedule are
not supplied. Enquiries download locally; they do not submit a booking.
Piano uses synthesized harmonics rather than sampled acoustic piano audio.
Visual score is an expressive trace, not a formal sheet-music transcription.
Browser tests used the available in-app Chromium browser; separate Safari,
Firefox, physical-device and screen-reader audits have not been performed.
